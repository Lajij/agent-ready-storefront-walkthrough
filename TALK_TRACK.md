# Talk track: Vercel Shop, the agent-ready storefront (Saltbush & Ridge)

**Length:** about 2.5 minutes · **Live demo:** https://agent-ready-storefront-walkthrough.vercel.app
Click through with the white buttons on each screen (or **Next →** / the right arrow key). All data is simulated; Saltbush & Ridge is a fictional company.

---

## 1. The new shopper (about 25s)
*Point at the tall white bar on the right of the chart.*

"This is Saltbush & Ridge, an Australian outdoor brand. Look at this chart. Six months ago, two percent of their online visits came from an AI assistant shopping on someone's behalf. Now it's eleven percent, eighteen thousand visits a month.

People convert at three point two percent. Those assistants? Under one percent. And in the reports they just look like bounced visits. The lost sales don't show up anywhere."

*Click "Watch an agent try to shop".*

## 2. Today: the agent guesses (about 25s)
*Click "Send the assistant shopping".*

"Here's why. A shopper asks for a light two-person tent under six hundred dollars, delivered to Hobart by Friday. The assistant lands on the product page, a page built for human eyes. It grabs the wrong price, the old one with the line through it. It can't see the stock, because that loads later. It gives up and sends the shopper to a marketplace.

The retailer had the right tent, at the right price, in stock. They lost the sale and never knew."

*Click "Now the same URL on Vercel Shop".*

## 3. One URL, two shoppers (about 30s)
*Click "Send the agent to the same URL".*

"This is what we solve with Vercel Shop, our storefront for Shopify. Same store, same product link. A person gets the beautiful product page on the left. An agent gets this on the right: the real price, what's in stock, the options, delivery. No guessing.

One customer put it perfectly: one store, two kinds of shoppers."

*Click "Let the agent shop".*

## 4. The agent shops (about 30s)
*Click "Play the conversation".*

"Now the assistant doesn't browse the site at all. The store gives it shopping tools at its own web address. Watch: it searches the catalog, reads the tent, checks it's in stock for Hobart, and comes back with an answer: five forty-nine, arrives Friday, and the matching footprint keeps you under six hundred. The shopper says yes, and the agent builds a real cart."

*Click "Hand it back to the shopper".*

## 5. Tap to confirm (about 20s)
*Click "Review and pay", then "Pay now".*

"The shopper stays in control. One tap, they review the cart and pay on Saltbush & Ridge's own checkout. And there it is, a real order, in their Shopify, from a shopper who arrived through an AI assistant."

*Click "What this is worth".*

## 6. What it's worth (about 25s)

"So what's it worth? These are illustrative numbers. Agent shoppers go from under one percent conversion to three point four. That's four hundred and sixty extra orders a month, almost a hundred thousand dollars a month, over a million a year, from shoppers they were already getting and losing.

That's the value: your store is ready for the way people are starting to shop. People and agents, one store, your checkout, your customer. And it's built on Shopify and runs on Vercel."
